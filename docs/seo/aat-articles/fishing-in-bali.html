Can you fish in Bali?

Yes. Charter operators work out of south Bali, and boats range from small traditional jukung to larger yachts. Tanjung Benoa is a peninsula in Badung Regency that's already known for water sports such as diving, parasailing and banana boats, so the boat infrastructure is there. We found no official Bali or national page on tourist angling, so ask the operator whether any area rules apply to your route.

Where do you go fishing in Bali?

South Bali is the default. Nusa Dua is a resort enclave built in the 1970s, about 22 kilometers from Denpasar, and Tanjung Benoa sits beside it. Other coasts have local fishing, but the charter market we could check is concentrated in the south.

What fish can you catch in Bali?

Operator listings name giant trevally, yellowfin tuna, dogtooth tuna, grouper and snapper. Giant trevally is the headline fish: it lives across the Indo-Pacific and can reach 170 centimeters and 80 kilograms, though most boats see far smaller ones. One FishingBooker report describes a first-trip catch of about 15 kilograms. Treat that as one customer's day, not an average.

When is the fishing season in Bali?

There's no official schedule that we could find. A FishingBooker guide says nearshore tuna fishing usually peaks around June, mostly mackerel tuna, with dogtooth and yellowfin as other local species. It names no month for giant trevally. Because the sources are commercial, ask your operator what they have been catching recently and plan a flexible day.

How much does a fishing charter in Bali cost?

On 9 November 2026 one Tanjung Benoa operator was listed on FishingBooker at Rp5,650,000 for a 4-hour trip and Rp8,750,000 for 8 hours. Prices vary with boat size, fuel and group size, so use these as a rough range and get a written quote.

What else do you pay as a visitor?

Foreign visitors to Bali pay a one-time levy of Rp150,000, according to the Bali Provincial Government. It's a visit fee, not a fishing fee; the Bali Provincial Government requires it before departure or during your stay.
