Phuket fishing mostly means a chartered boat. You join a shared trip or book a private boat, head out into the Andaman Sea, and troll or jig for big fish such as marlin, tuna and sailfish. Most day trips leave from Chalong Pier on the island's south side.

Can you fish in Phuket?

Yes. Many operators list full-day big game and trolling trips from Phuket. The boat crew supplies the tackle and usually lunch, and hotel pickup is common. We couldn't find a Thai Department of Fisheries or TAT page that sets out rules for tourists on a charter, so let the operator confirm what permits or park rules apply on the day you go.

Where do Phuket fishing trips go?

Listings for Racha Yai island, south of Phuket, leave from Chalong Pier, and the trip is a day on open water, not a few hours near the shore. Phuket province is the main island plus 32 smaller islands in the Andaman Sea, so boats have plenty of sea to cover. Which grounds you fish depends on weather and the captain's read of the day.

What fish can you catch off Phuket?

Operators advertise marlin, tuna and sailfish. The Indo-Pacific sailfish is the species found across the Indian and Pacific Oceans, so it's a realistic target in these waters. Don't treat any of these as a promise. Listings describe what the boat is searching for, and some trips catch smaller fish instead.

When is the best time to go fishing in Phuket?

That depends on who you ask. One guide gives November to April as the high season, while a listing we saw says February to April. We couldn't find an official calendar, so we won't pick one. Ask the operator which fish they're finding in your travel month, and check the sea forecast, because rough water cancels trips whatever the season.

How much does a Phuket fishing charter cost?

Platform listings seen on 9 November 2026 start at about 3,800 baht for a shared full-day big game trip, and about 3,500 baht for a spectator place on the boat. A private boat for one to four people was listed at around 14,000 baht, per Pelago. These are listing prices, not quotes. Hotel transfer fees, a minimum group size and weather cancellation terms differ by listing, so read them before you pay.

Is it a good trip for everyone?

Listings warn that anyone who is pregnant, or has back, neck or joint problems, shouldn't join, because hauling a large fish is physical and the boat moves. Non-fishing companions can sometimes come as spectators.

Pairing a fishing day with a Phuket stay

A full day out leaves little energy for sightseeing, so book the boat for a day you have nothing else planned.
